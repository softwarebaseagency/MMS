A **Membership Management System** should help any membership-based business manage members, subscriptions, payments, access, renewals, engagement, reports, and offers in one organized platform.

## 1. Member Registration

Allow staff to register new members with full name, phone number, email, address, date of birth, gender if needed, emergency contact, and joining date.

## 2. Member Profiles

Create a complete profile for each member showing personal details, membership status, payment history, attendance history, notes, documents, and assigned plan.

## 3. Membership Plans

Create different membership plans such as monthly, quarterly, yearly, family, student, corporate, VIP, trial, or custom plans.

## 4. Subscription Management

Track active subscriptions, start dates, end dates, renewals, freezes, upgrades, downgrades, cancellations, and expired memberships.

## 5. Membership Status Tracking

Show clear member statuses such as active, inactive, expired, suspended, frozen, cancelled, trial, pending payment, or blacklisted.

## 6. Automatic Renewal Reminders

Send reminders before membership expiry so members and staff can renew on time.

## 7. Payment Tracking

Record payments, partial payments, unpaid balances, advance payments, refunds, discounts, and payment dates.

## 8. Invoice and Receipt Generation

Generate invoices and receipts for membership payments, renewals, upgrades, extra services, and penalties.

## 9. Cash and Payment Method Management

Track payment methods such as cash, card, bank transfer, wallet, credit account, or mixed payment depending on the business policy.

## 10. Access Control

Control member access based on active membership, expired status, unpaid balance, suspended account, or special permission.

## 11. Check-in / Attendance Tracking

Record member visits through manual check-in, QR code, barcode, card scan, phone number, or biometric integration if required.

## 12. Visit History

Store each member’s visit history, including date, time, location, staff user, and access result.

## 13. Attendance Statistics

Show how often members visit, who is most active, who stopped attending, and which days or times have the highest activity.

## 14. Member Engagement Tracking

Identify members who have not visited recently, members close to expiry, members with unpaid balances, and members likely to cancel.

## 15. Lead and Prospect Management

Track potential members, inquiries, follow-ups, trial users, source of lead, assigned salesperson, and conversion status.

## 16. Trial Memberships

Create trial memberships with limited access, expiry dates, conversion tracking, and follow-up reminders.

## 17. Family / Group Memberships

Support multiple members under one account, shared billing, group discounts, and linked member profiles.

## 18. Corporate Memberships

Manage company accounts, employee memberships, corporate rates, company billing, and usage reports.

## 19. Discounts and Promotions

Create promotional offers, seasonal discounts, referral rewards, renewal deals, loyalty offers, and special pricing.

## 20. Deal and Offer Management

Create targeted deals for specific member groups such as inactive members, long-term members, new members, expiring members, or high-value members.

## 21. Referral Program

Track referrals, reward members who bring new customers, and report referral performance.

## 22. Loyalty Program

Manage points, rewards, member levels, benefits, vouchers, and loyalty-based discounts.

## 23. Member Segmentation

Group members by status, plan type, payment behavior, attendance, location, age group, lead source, renewal history, or activity level.

## 24. Automated Notifications

Send SMS, WhatsApp, email, or in-system notifications for expiry reminders, payment reminders, offers, announcements, renewals, and follow-ups.

## 25. Communication History

Store all messages, calls, notes, reminders, and follow-ups related to each member.

## 26. Staff Task Management

Assign staff tasks such as follow-ups, renewals, payment collection, complaint handling, and member support.

## 27. Notes and Internal Comments

Allow staff to add private notes to member profiles for service quality, follow-up history, special requests, or warnings.

## 28. Appointment / Session Booking

Allow members or staff to book appointments, sessions, consultations, services, or scheduled activities if the business requires it.

## 29. Calendar Management

Show bookings, appointments, renewals, staff schedules, events, and important member-related dates in calendar view.

## 30. Waiting List

Manage waiting lists for limited-capacity plans, sessions, services, or events.

## 31. Capacity Management

Track capacity limits for locations, rooms, sessions, events, or service slots.

## 32. Product and Service Sales

Allow selling additional products, services, packages, add-ons, or one-time charges linked to the member account.

## 33. Add-ons Management

Create optional add-ons such as extra services, premium access, special privileges, consultations, or bundled offers.

## 34. Freezing / Pausing Memberships

Allow memberships to be paused for a specific period with reason, approval, and automatic new expiry calculation.

## 35. Membership Transfer

Allow transferring a membership to another person or branch with permission control and audit history.

## 36. Cancellation Management

Record cancellation requests, cancellation reasons, final balance, refund decision, and retention attempts.

## 37. Retention Management

Track members at risk of leaving, create follow-up tasks, offer retention deals, and measure saved memberships.

## 38. Feedback and Complaints

Record member feedback, complaints, ratings, suggestions, issue status, and resolution history.

## 39. Surveys

Send surveys to understand member satisfaction, service quality, pricing opinion, and improvement needs.

## 40. Dashboard

Show total members, active members, expired members, new members, renewals, revenue, unpaid balances, attendance, retention rate, and conversion rate.

## 41. Reports and Analytics

Generate reports for membership growth, revenue, renewals, cancellations, attendance, offers, staff performance, member activity, and payment collection.

## 42. Revenue Analytics

Track revenue by plan, branch, staff member, payment method, period, promotion, member type, or service category.

## 43. Renewal Analytics

Show renewal rates, upcoming expirations, expired memberships, late renewals, and renewal performance by staff or plan.

## 44. Cancellation Analytics

Track why members cancel, when they cancel, which plans have the highest cancellation rate, and which groups need attention.

## 45. Offer Performance Reports

Measure which discounts, campaigns, referral programs, and retention offers produce the best results.

## 46. Lead Conversion Reports

Track how many inquiries become members, conversion rate by source, salesperson performance, and follow-up success.

## 47. Staff Management

Manage staff accounts, roles, permissions, departments, assigned tasks, performance, and activity history.

## 48. User Roles and Permissions

Control access for admin, manager, sales, accountant, receptionist, service staff, and viewer roles.

## 49. Multi-Branch Support

Support multiple locations with branch-level members, staff, payments, access rules, reports, and centralized management.

## 50. Member Portal

Allow members to view their membership status, expiry date, payment history, invoices, bookings, offers, and announcements.

## 51. Mobile Access

Allow staff and members to access the system from mobile, tablet, or desktop.

## 52. Document Uploads

Upload contracts, ID documents, signed forms, consent forms, receipts, certificates, or any member-related files.

## 53. Contract and Agreement Management

Generate or upload membership agreements, terms and conditions, consent forms, cancellation forms, and signed documents.

## 54. Digital Signature

Allow members to digitally sign agreements, consent forms, policy documents, or renewal confirmations.

## 55. Rules and Policy Management

Set rules for renewals, refunds, freezing, cancellation, access, late payments, transfer, discounts, and member conduct.

## 56. Penalty and Fee Management

Apply fees for late payments, cancellations, missed bookings, damaged items, or policy violations if applicable.

## 57. Inventory / Resource Tracking

Track cards, equipment, uniforms, products, consumables, or assigned resources if the business provides physical items to members.

## 58. QR Code / Barcode Membership Card

Generate digital or printed membership cards with QR codes, barcodes, or member IDs.

## 59. Access Device Integration

Integrate with turnstiles, doors, card readers, QR scanners, biometric devices, or other access hardware if needed.

## 60. Import and Export

Import member data from Excel or CSV and export reports, member lists, invoices, attendance, and payment data.

## 61. Search and Filtering

Search members by name, phone number, member ID, status, plan, branch, payment status, expiry date, lead source, or activity level.

## 62. Activity Log

Track who created, edited, deleted, renewed, froze, cancelled, checked in, discounted, or changed a member account.

## 63. Security and Backup

Protect member and payment data with secure login, encrypted storage, backups, permissions, and data recovery.

## 64. Data Privacy Controls

Control access to sensitive member information and keep clear records for privacy, consent, and compliance.

## 65. Automated Workflows

Automate common processes such as welcome messages, renewal reminders, inactive member follow-ups, payment reminders, birthday messages, and post-cancellation follow-ups.

## 66. Custom Fields

Allow businesses to add custom fields depending on their industry, such as member type, source, assigned advisor, preferred service, membership reason, or special requirements.

## 67. Branch Transfer

Move a member between branches while keeping payment history, attendance history, and membership records.

## 68. Blacklist / Warning List

Flag members who should be restricted because of unpaid balances, repeated violations, misuse, or management decision.

## 69. Announcements

Send general announcements to all members or selected groups based on branch, status, plan, or activity.

## 70. Backup Export and Archive

Archive old memberships, cancelled members, historical payments, and activity records while keeping them searchable.

The most important requirements are **member profiles, subscription tracking, renewal reminders, payment management, attendance tracking, member segmentation, targeted offers, reports, permissions, and automated follow-ups**.
